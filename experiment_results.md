## Prompt C — Experiment

**Date:** 2026-10-23 13:39

### Prompt Used
Act as a creative startup mentor for college students.

Example:
Name: CampusSwap
Problem: Students have unused textbooks.
Solution: A student marketplace for exchanging
or selling used textbooks.
Difference: It focuses on the campus community.

Now generate 5 NEW startup ideas for college students.

For each idea, include its name, problem, solution,
target users, unique feature, and first testing step.

Make the ideas practical, original, and easy to understand.
Do not copy the example.

### Actual AI Output
1. QueueLess — Smart Campus Queue Manager
- Problem: Students waste time waiting in queues at college canteens, libraries, and administrative offices.
- Solution: A simple website where students can check queue lengths and reserve a time slot.
- Target users: College students, canteen staff, and college administration.
- Unique feature: Students can report live waiting times, helping others choose less crowded times.
- First testing step: Ask one college canteen to test a Google Form-based ordering or queue system with 20 students.
2. SkillCircle — Student Skill Exchange
- Problem: Students want to learn new skills but cannot always afford paid courses.
- Solution: A platform where students teach each other skills in exchange for learning another skill.
- Target users: College students interested in coding, graphic design, video editing, communication, and other skills.
- Unique feature: Students earn learning credits by teaching, then use those credits to attend sessions.
- First testing step: Find 10 students with different skills and organize five free skill-exchange sessions.
3. EventEase — College Event Organizer
- Problem: College clubs struggle to manage registrations, attendance, schedules, and event updates.
- Solution: A simple event-management website for creating events, registering participants, and tracking attendance.
- Target users: College clubs, student coordinators, and event participants.
- Unique feature: Each event gets a QR-code check-in system and a live participant count.
- First testing step: Help one college club organize a small event using a basic registration form and QR codes.
4. FreshStart — Fresher's Campus Guide
- Problem: New students often struggle to understand college facilities, important processes, and campus life.
- Solution: A digital guide with campus maps, useful contacts, FAQs, and tips from senior students.
- Target users: First-year students, new admissions, and student mentors.
- Unique feature: Personalized checklists that help freshers complete important tasks during their first month.
- First testing step: Interview 10 first-year students and create a simple guide covering their five most common questions.
5. GreenPoints — Eco-Friendly Campus Rewards
- Problem: Students may want to recycle waste and reduce plastic use but lack motivation and convenient options.
- Solution: A campus program that rewards students with points for verified eco-friendly activities.
- Target users: College students, environmental clubs, and campus administrators.
- Unique feature: Students earn points for activities such as participating in clean-up drives or using approved recycling stations.
- First testing step: Partner with a campus environmental club to run a one-week recycling challenge and track participation using a simple form.END
